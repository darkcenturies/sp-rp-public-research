# Research scope and rights

This directory intentionally publishes the completed generated decompilation/disassembly archive and reconstructed interface pseudocode for the identified targets. The project owner's publication instruction supersedes the earlier rule that generated research must remain private.

Generated and reconstructed third-party material is not covered or relicensed by the root BSD license. Underlying third-party rights remain with their respective holders. Research output is not the original authored vendor source, and publication does not constitute a third-party permission grant.

The project remains unofficial and unaffiliated with the named vendors. Input game/client/server executables, commercial game assets, private gamemode code, player records, production settings and secrets are not included. Maintain attribution and distinguish original tooling from derived output.
